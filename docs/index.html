FORE.
Wrong turn.

Aim by dragging Google Street View. Pick a club, set the power, then fire a golf ball into the real world. One swing is one stroke. Your next Street View position is where the ball lands.

HOW TO PLAY

Aim and fire through buildings.

You start on outdoor Google Street View with a hidden pin somewhere nearby. There is no route line. You get distance and bearing; everything else is judgement.

01

Aim

Drag the panorama until the centre reticle points where you want to hit. Looking around costs nothing.

02

Swing

Choose Driver, Iron or Wedge, set the power, and hit SWING (or Space). The ball visibly flies down your chosen line.

03

Land

The game moves Street View to the nearest outdoor panorama around the calculated landing point. One swing = one stroke.

⛳

Hole out

Get within 35 metres of the hidden pin. Nine holes make a round; lowest score wins.
